About

Background
Livery design, built the way a real fleet graphics shop would build it.

I'm Boee, a livery designer at PGraphix Studio. I design full vehicle liveries for lore-friendly emergency departments — sheriff's offices, highway patrol, and everything in between — start to finish, from reference research to the final game-ready texture file.

Every project starts the same way: study how the real-world department (or the one it's inspired by) actually lays out its graphics, then rebuild that logic — badges, striping, door copy, unit numbers — panel by panel against the vehicle's own template.
